### Post 2

Most feeds optimize for engagement.

Timeline starts with your policy: topics, coverage briefs, preferred depth, source standards, exclusions, and feedback.

You decide what deserves attention before an algorithm fills the screen.

### Post 3

Timeline Curator runs inside Codex—on demand or on a schedule.

For every cycle it searches from multiple angles, samples broadly, verifies important claims, inspects sources, looks for attributable media, and publishes only stories that clear your policy.

### Post 4

The result is a visual, evidence-backed feed:

• concise summaries  
• why each story matters  
• inspected sources  
• attributed images or videos  
• feedback choices tailored to each story

And a text-only story is still allowed when it is genuinely important.

### Post 5

Timeline learns from explicit signals—not silent engagement guesses.

Tell it when a story is useful, stale, too shallow, already known, from a strong source, or simply not for you. Your next curation cycle receives that context.

### Post 6

Stories can be shared through WhatsApp, X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Telegram, Reddit, Threads, Bluesky, email, or your device share sheet.

Shared pages contain the story and sources—not your identity, private policy, or feedback.
